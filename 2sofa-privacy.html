Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Your saved home and chosen sofa stay on your phone. 2Sofa has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated backend. Address searches and actions involving Google Maps can send information to external services, as explained below.

Information stored on your phone

2Sofa stores the latitude and longitude of your chosen home (HOME), your selected sofa appearance and whether you have acknowledged the introductory information. These settings are held in the app’s private storage. The app’s settings are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup.

Entered addresses, search results and imported place details are used to help you choose HOME. They are not kept as an address book or search history. Search or import details may temporarily remain in the app’s screen state while a dialog is open. A replacement HOME is saved only after you confirm it; your previous HOME remains until then.

Your device location

With your Android permission, 2Sofa accesses your approximate or precise location while the app is open to calculate the distance to HOME. When you choose “I’m home now”, a sufficiently precise location is saved as HOME. The app does not request background location access and stops location updates when it is no longer in the foreground. It does not store a location history.

You can refuse or revoke location permission in Android’s app settings. You can still set HOME through an address search or a place shared from Google Maps. Location-dependent features need location access; precise location is required to save HOME using your current position. Android’s own location services may communicate with their providers according to your device settings and those providers’ policies.

Address searches

When you press the address-search button, the entered address is submitted to your phone’s Android Geocoder service to obtain matching addresses and coordinates. The provider depends on your device and is often Google. The provider may receive the address, the requested language, your IP address and technical connection information. The developer does not receive these searches. A result becomes HOME only when you select and confirm it.

Google Maps routes and shared places

  • Opening a route: the route button opens Google Maps in its app or in a browser. 2Sofa passes your saved HOME coordinates and, if a recent location is available, your current coordinates to request a public transport route. Google Maps may also use its own location permissions, account settings, cookies and activity controls.
  • Searching in Maps: choosing the Google Maps search alternative sends your entered search text to Google Maps.
  • Importing a shared place: 2Sofa reads the text or Maps link you explicitly share with it. If exact coordinates are already contained in the shared text or link, they can be read on your phone. Otherwise, the app may connect to Google to resolve the link and obtain the place coordinates. Google receives the requested link, your IP address and technical request information. The place is saved as HOME only after your confirmation.

Google processes information received by its services under the Google Privacy Policy. A different Geocoder or location provider may have its own policy. 2Sofa does not receive or control the providers’ account history or server logs.

Purposes and legal grounds

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, processing needed to provide the functions you request, including saving HOME, looking up an address and opening a route, is based on Article 6(1)(b). Optional device-location access is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a). You can withdraw this consent for future access through Android’s permission settings. Withdrawing permission does not itself delete HOME or reverse information already sent to a service.

If you contact the developer, your email address and the information you provide are processed to answer your request, based on the legitimate interest in responding to enquiries under Article 6(1)(f), and where applicable to meet legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c). The contact mailbox is provided by Gmail, a Google service. Acknowledging the introductory page is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

Retention and deletion

HOME and app preferences remain on your phone until you replace them, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. The developer has no server-side copy of HOME to retrieve or delete. Current location readings and search/import results are temporary, apart from the coordinates you choose to save as HOME.

Contact correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any applicable legal obligations. External services determine their own retention periods. Clearing 2Sofa’s storage or uninstalling it does not delete data held by Google, your location-service provider or GitHub; use their privacy controls or contact them for such requests.

External providers and international processing

Google, the location-service provider on your device and GitHub may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices explain applicable legal grounds, international-transfer safeguards, retention and privacy controls. For Google and GitHub, see the linked notices below; for another device provider, consult its privacy information. These providers are responsible for their own service processing. The developer does not operate a database of your app location or address searches.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions of applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation, and withdraw consent for the future. Contact the developer using the email address at the bottom of this page. For information held only by an external provider, you may also contact that provider directly.

You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority. 2Sofa does not carry out automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects or profile you for advertising.

This website and policy updates

This English-language page is hosted on GitHub Pages and can be read without an account. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security and processes technical visit information under its privacy statement. This page contains no analytics, advertising, embedded maps, external fonts or tracking scripts added by the developer. Opening links to other websites is subject to those websites’ policies.

This policy may be updated when the app or its processing changes. The date at the top identifies the current version.
