Privacy notice

Updated: 26 September 2026

Data we process

Name, search term, place, enquiry, language, contacts found, favourites and settings are processed and partly stored on your device. The app passes the email draft to your email app. You send it yourself. The app does not read your mailbox. No account is required.

Search sends the search term, place and coordinates through Supabase to Brave Search. For place search, komoot (Photon) receives the entered place. Your name and the email text are not transmitted for this search. If an app offers device location, we use it only at your request and with permission. You can enter a place instead.

If the app generates the email draft for you, your name, enquiry, place, language and details of the provider found are sent through Supabase to OpenAI.

When loading a map, OpenStreetMap receives the requested map area. If an app shows photos, it loads them from the respective image provider. To find email addresses, the app retrieves public websites. These services receive your IP address and technical request data.

Supabase stores the time of each search so that monthly limits and a brake against too many requests can be applied. Your name and enquiry are not stored for this. Some apps may additionally store a random installation identifier. If an app offers a paid plan, Google Play handles purchase and payment. The app and backend then check access using the purchase identifier and status. We do not receive full payment details.

Purposes and legal bases

Providing the features you request and, where an app offers them, paid plans is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Optional location use relies on consent, withdrawable at any time for the future, under Article 6(1)(a). Security and abuse prevention serve our legitimate interest in secure operation under Article 6(1)(f). Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c). A feature cannot be provided without the data necessary for it. Location permission is optional.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until changed or cleared with app data; caches may be overwritten. Search timestamps on the server remain until you request deletion. A new month resets only the count used for the limit. Where an app stores an installation identifier or subscription records, there is currently no automatic deletion period. You can request deletion using the contact above; evidence identifying the installation or purchase may be needed. Statutory retention obligations remain applicable.

Technical logs of Supabase and OpenAI follow the retention rules of the service in use. According to its privacy notice, Brave may retain queries for billing and troubleshooting for up to 90 days, subject to legal obligations. Deleting the app does not remove emails held by your provider or cancel a Google Play subscription.

Providers and international processing

The primary Supabase database is in London. The providers mentioned may also process data outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices describe the relevant transfers and safeguards. Contact us for information about safeguards used for our processing.

Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent for the future. Contact us using the address above. You can complain to a supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
