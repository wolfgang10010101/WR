2you

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

2you prepares a personal WhatsApp message with a route home or to your pickup location. No account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated backend. The developer does not receive your app settings, contacts or location.

Data on your phone

The app saves your home coordinates and address label, the selected recipient’s name and phone number, your two message texts, travel mode and WhatsApp preference in private app storage. These settings are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. They remain until you change them, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Location, addresses and contacts

With your Android permission, 2you obtains approximate or precise location only when you save your current position as home or prepare a message using a button or widget arrow. It uses Google Play location services, requests no background location permission and keeps no location history. You can refuse or revoke permission in Android settings; preparing a route then requires you to grant access again.

Address searches use your device’s Android Geocoder. The app may also ask it for an address matching a position you choose as home. Its provider, often Google, may receive the address or coordinates, your IP address and technical request data. Your location-service provider may process data under your device settings. The developer receives none of these requests.

The Android contact picker gives the app access only to the phone entry you select. 2you does not request permission to read your entire address book. You can enter the recipient manually. The app checks only whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is available to handle your message.

WhatsApp and Google Maps

Opening WhatsApp passes the recipient’s phone number, your message and a Google Maps link containing your current and home coordinates to WhatsApp. You confirm sending in WhatsApp. The route runs from your location to home, or from home to your pickup location. The coordinates represent that moment, not live tracking.

WhatsApp may process the link to create a preview. When someone opens it, Google Maps receives the route information and normal connection data. These services process information under their own policies: WhatsApp and Google. 2you does not read your conversations or receive delivery reports.

This website and external services

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes IP addresses and technical visit data under its privacy statement. This page has no developer-added cookies, tracking scripts, external fonts or embedded maps. External providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; their notices explain retention, privacy controls and international-transfer safeguards. Removing 2you does not delete data already held by them or by message recipients.

Your choices and rights

Where GDPR applies, processing needed for the functions you request is based on Article 6(1)(b), and optional location access on your consent under Article 6(1)(a). You can withdraw permission for future access in Android settings. Withdrawing permission does not delete saved settings or recall a message.

If you contact the developer, your email and correspondence are used to answer your enquiry under Article 6(1)(f), retained only as long as needed for that enquiry and applicable legal obligations. The mailbox uses Gmail, covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and withdraw consent. Contact the person below; for data held by an external provider, contact that provider. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority. 2you makes no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

This policy will be updated if the app’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this version.
