dejax

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

dejax does not send saved place records or visit counts to the developer. It has no account, ads, analytics or developer-operated server. The developer receives no location or visit records from the app. You can manually export a backup and choose where to save it.

What dejax stores

In private app storage, dejax keeps the names and coordinates of your saved places, their recognition radius, visit counts, start dates, recent and previous arrival dates, and entry/exit timestamps and state needed to avoid duplicate counts. If you enable new-place detection, dejax also stores coordinates and times of potential stays and your distance and duration settings locally. The widget shows the number of suggestions awaiting review. dejax does not record a continuous route history or upload these records. App data is excluded from Android cloud and device-transfer backups.

Location permission

Precise location is used when you save a place. With background-location permission, Android and Google Play services geofencing detect arrivals and departures even when dejax is closed. Optional new-place detection uses occasional background location checks to identify longer stays away from known places. You can switch it off in the app. You can refuse or revoke permission in Android settings; automatic counting then pauses, but saved records remain.

Android and Google Play services may use network-assisted location and process information under your device settings and their own policies. The developer does not receive this information or control those services.

Maps

When you open a map, dejax requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap. The provider receives your IP address, technical request details and the requested map area; saved place names and visit counts are not sent. Tiles are cached locally for reuse. No address or place-name lookup is performed. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting records

Unconfirmed suggestions expire after seven days; dismissed areas are remembered for up to 24 hours to avoid repeated suggestions. These suggestions are not included in manual backups. Saved place records stay on your phone until you delete a place, clear dejax’s app storage or uninstall it. Deleting a place also removes its count and visit dates. There is no developer-held copy to recover. Removing local records does not delete exported backup files or information independently held by a device-location provider.

Manual backups

To move to a new phone, you can create a JSON backup of your places, coordinates, counts and visit dates and restore it in dejax. Android’s system file picker lets you choose a folder, USB storage or a storage provider you use. dejax does not upload or automatically transfer the backup. If you choose a cloud provider, that provider handles storage under its own terms and privacy policy. The file is not encrypted by dejax and contains personal places: keep it safe and delete exported copies yourself when no longer needed. Restoring replaces the current places and counts after your confirmation; it does not count as a visit.

Purposes, contact and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, saving and displaying the records you request is based on Article 6(1)(b); optional location access is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a). You can withdraw permission for future access at any time. dejax does not make automated decisions with legal effects or create advertising profiles.

If you email the developer, your email address and message are used to answer your enquiry, based on Article 6(1)(f), and kept only as long as needed for that enquiry or legal obligations. The mailbox uses Gmail. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and complain to a supervisory authority such as the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
